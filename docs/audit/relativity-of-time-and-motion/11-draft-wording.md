## 3. Words and formulas (new box, collapsed, above Part 1)
- **Event:** one thing happening at one place and one moment. **Position:** where it happens, in light-seconds. **Time:** when it happens, in seconds. **Simultaneous / the same moment:** happening at the same time.
- **Frame:** the point of view of someone standing still in their own group, with their own clocks and rulers. **Lab:** where the experiment is set up. The block's own frame is the point of view of someone riding with the block.
- **Axis:** a line that shows which direction to measure along, like the lines on graph paper. A frame has two: one for when (time) and one for where (space). **Time axis:** the line along which a frame's time runs. For the lab it points straight up. For the block, it is the path of a spot that stays at the block's centre as time passes, and it leans because the block is moving. **Space axis:** a frame's own "same moment" line. For the lab it points straight across. For the block it is the tilted line from "Same time, different line". **Origin:** the point where the flash is released, position 0 and time 0.
- **Light-second:** the distance light travels in one second, about 300,000 km. **c:** the speed of light; 0.5c is half of it.
- **Time dilation factor:** how much slower a moving clock ticks than the lab's, as a fraction ("The moving clock"). At 0.5c it is 0.866.
- **Lorentz transformation:** the rule that turns an event's lab position and time into the position and time a moving frame would give for the same event.
- **Formula 1, the block's own time = (1 ÷ time dilation factor) × (the lab's time − speed × the lab's position).** Example (not the answer to the guess): an event at position 0.2 light-seconds and time 0.5 s, seen from a frame moving at 0.6c. The factor is 0.8, so 1 ÷ 0.8 = 1.25, and 1.25 × (0.5 − 0.6 × 0.2) = **0.475 s**.
- **Formula 2, the block's own position = (1 ÷ time dilation factor) × (the lab's position − speed × the lab's time).** The same example: 1.25 × (0.2 − 0.6 × 0.5) = **−0.125 light-seconds**.
- *(Withheld until after the run, because they are the answer to the guess: the 0.5c block times.)*
